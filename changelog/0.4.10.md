# 0.4.10 - 2026-10-21

The perceptual gate the lossy model pipeline will stand on, and a lint that says what is
actually wrong with a single model.

### Added

- `model lint` and `model_lint`, a standalone scan of one .mdx or .mdl. Finite vertices,
  normals and UVs, zero-length normals, indices past the vertex array, zero-area triangles,
  skin bones the skeleton does not define, weight sets that do not sum to one, non-finite
  animation keys, and textures no geoset references. Read-only, and it exits 1 on a finding.
- `model compare` and `model_compare`, which render two models from four angles and score
  SSIM and silhouette intersection-over-union per angle, plus a mean and a worst, so a lossy
  step can be accepted or refused on a measured number instead of a guess.
- `AppearanceMetric` in Wc3.Render, the SSIM and silhouette score over the union of the two
  masks, so an empty background cannot flatter a shrinking model.

### Changed

- The model texture resolver used by thumbnails is shared with the comparison, so a standalone
  model resolves textures from its own folder exactly as a thumbnail does.
